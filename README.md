## Watch, Read & Physical

### Streaming

- [HIDIVE](https://www.hidive.com/season/20022) - Official North American streaming for *Tamako Market* (sub & dub, region-dependent); see Sentai catalog for [*Tamako Love Story*](https://www.sentaifilmworks.com/blogs/catalog/tamako-love-story).
- [Bilibili — *Tamako Market*](https://www.bilibili.com/bangumi/play/ss4262) · [*Tamako Love Story*](https://www.bilibili.com/bangumi/play/ss4155) - Official Simplified Chinese licensed streaming (region-dependent). `[ZH]`
- [d Anime Store — *Tamako Market*](https://animestore.docomo.ne.jp/animestore/ci_pc?workId=20325) · [*Tamako Love Story*](https://animestore.docomo.ne.jp/animestore/ci_pc?workId=23448) - Official Japanese subscription streaming (Japan only). `[JA]`

### Novels & Official Books

- [KA Esuma Bunko — *Tamako Market* Novel](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/books/tamako/market/) ([Catalog](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/books/lineup/?cd=978-4-907064-03-7)) - Official light novel by Mutsuki Ichinose, supervised by Reiko Yoshida and illustrated by Yukiko Horiguchi (ISBN `978-4-907064-03-7`; 11 original stories). `[JA]`
- [KA Esuma Bunko — *Tamako Love Story* Novel](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/books/tamako/) ([Catalog](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/books/lineup/?cd=978-4-907064-22-8)) - Film novelization spanning Tamako, Midori, and an original Choi story (ISBN `978-4-907064-22-8`). `[JA]`
- [Official Books & Guidebooks](https://tamakolovestory.com/product/book/) - Details the 100-page *Illustration & Setting Materials* book and official full-color guidebook *TAMAKO MEMORY'S NOTE* (ISBN `978-4-907064-28-0`; staff/cast interviews and epilogue short story; [Official PV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ky1LGb_4NNc)). `[JA]`

## Sakuga, Interviews & Criticism

- [Sakugabooru — `tamako_market`](https://www.sakugabooru.com/post?tags=tamako_market) · [`tamako_love_story`](https://www.sakugabooru.com/post?tags=tamako_love_story) - Indexed key animation cuts across TV and film.

- [Sakuga Blog — *TAMAKO MEMORY'S NOTE* Interviews: Naoko Yamada](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2016/11/21/tamako-memorys-note-interview-naoko-yamada/) · [Reiko Yoshida](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2016/12/05/tamako-memorys-note-interview-reiko-yoshida/) - Translated guidebook interviews on structure and storytelling.
- [Sakuga Blog — Film Pamphlet Interviews: Yamada × Aya Suzaki](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2016/10/10/tamako-love-story-special-talk-naoko-yamada-x-aya-suzaki/) · [Reiko Yoshida](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2016/10/17/tamako-love-story-pamphlet-interview-reiko-yoshida/) · [Yukiko Horiguchi](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2016/10/24/tamako-love-story-pamphlet-interview-yukiko-horiguchi/) - Translated theater booklet interviews with the core trio and lead voice actress.
- [Sakuga Blog — Stage Greetings: Yamada × Ryousuke Takahashi](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2016/11/07/tamako-love-story-stage-greeting-naoko-yamada-ryousuke-takahashi/) · [Yamada × Futoshi Nishiya × Taichi Ogawa](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2016/12/19/tamako-stage-greetings-naoko-yamada-x-futoshi-nishiya-x-taichi-ogawa/) - Translated talks on direction and Kyoto Animation craft.
- [Official Film Site — Naoko Yamada Interview](https://tamakolovestory.com/special/interview/) ([English Translation](https://ultimatemegax.wordpress.com/2014/03/08/tamako-love-story-special-director-yamada-interview-translated/)) - Two-part official interview on portraying Tamako at seventeen. `[JA]`
- [Anime!Anime! — Naoko Yamada Award Interview](https://animeanime.jp/article/2015/01/29/21773.html) ([English Translation](https://ultimatemegax.wordpress.com/2015/01/31/tamako-love-storydirector-naoko-yamada-interview-media-arts-festival-ver/)) - Media Arts Festival interview on framing, lighting, and sound design. `[JA]`
- [Wrong Every Time — *Our Aimless, Priceless Days*](https://wrongeverytime.com/2016/09/16/our-aimless-priceless-days-tamako-love-story/) - Close critical reading of *Tamako Love Story*'s framing and coming-of-age tone.
- [All the Anime — *Tamako Market*](https://blog.alltheanime.com/tamako-market/) - Essay by Andrew Osmond examining the TV series, film sequel, and *K-On!* lineage.
- [Anime News Network — TV Blu-ray Review](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/review/tamako-market/blu-ray-complete-collection/.97766) · [*Tamako Love Story* Review](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/shelf-life/2017-09-04/.120887) - Critical reviews of both releases.
- [OTACTURE — Aesthetics of *Tamako Love Story*](https://ukkah.hatenadiary.org/entry/20140503/p1) - Japanese essay on Yamada's camera language and visual sensibility. `[JA]`
